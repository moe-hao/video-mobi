2 · How to cancel (in app)

Cancel your subscription

Subscriptions can be cancelled at any time in the Vivid Arc Show app.

  1. Open the app and tap Profile at the bottom.
  2. Tap Feedback, then the Payment tab.
  3. Tap Cancel Subscription.
  4. Tap Submit to confirm.
